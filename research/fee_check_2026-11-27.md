These quotes already include pool fees, Jupiter's 0.1% fee each way (`feeBps` 10) and price impact. Not included: the network fee (5,000 lamports per signature plus a priority fee, about $0.001 to $0.07 per swap, an estimate), token-account rent of 0.00203928 SOL (about $0.15) on the first buy of a coin (refunded only if the account is closed), and slippage or price movement during a real trade.

Realistic all-in: about $0.45 to $0.90 per $20 round trip (2% to 4.5%), versus the $1.80 (9%) in settings.json.

Jupiter's `priceImpact` is measured against its own reference price and does not track this cost well (ROCKET and CINU show a positive buy impact). The bot therefore gates real buys on the measured round trip (`live_max_roundtrip_loss_pct`), not on `priceImpact`.

Quotes change minute to minute. Re-measure the current watchlist with `python -m memebot fees --data data`.
